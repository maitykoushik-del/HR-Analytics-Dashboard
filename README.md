# HR Analytics Dashboard Using Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard
developed using Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard provides insights into employee demographics,
workforce distribution, employee attrition, and satisfaction.

It helps users explore HR data and understand employee
turnover patterns across different categories.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze total employees and employees who left.
- Calculate the employee attrition rate.
- Analyze attrition by gender and department.
- Compare attrition across job roles.
- Understand attrition across different age groups.
- Analyze employee satisfaction and job levels.
- Present key HR metrics in an interactive dashboard.

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Excel Slicers
- KPI Cards
- Data Visualization

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Employees: 4,410
- Employees Left: 711
- Attrition Rate: 16%
- Average Monthly Income: 65,029
- Average Age: 37
- Average Tenure: 7 Years

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Employee Overview
Displays total employees, employees who left,
attrition rate, average monthly income, average age,
and average tenure.

### 2. Attrition by Gender
Compares employee attrition between male and female employees.

### 3. Attrition by Department
Analyzes employee attrition across departments.

### 4. Attrition by Job Role
Shows attrition patterns across different job roles.

### 5. Attrition by Education Field
Compares employee attrition across education fields.

### 6. Attrition by Age Group
Analyzes employee attrition across different age groups.

### 7. Attrition by Job Level
Compares employee attrition across job levels.

### 8. Satisfaction Analysis
Presents the distribution of employee satisfaction levels.

## Interactive Features

- Department slicer
- Job role slicer
- Marital status slicer
- Business travel slicer
- Gender slicer

These interactive filters allow users to explore
different segments of the workforce.

## Key Insights

- The dashboard reports 4,410 total employees.
- A total of 711 employees have left.
- The displayed attrition rate is 16%.
- The average employee age is 37 years.
- Average employee tenure is 7 years.
- Attrition can be explored across departments,
  job roles, education fields, and age groups.
