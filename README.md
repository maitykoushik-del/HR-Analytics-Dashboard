# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard provides insights into employee
workforce demographics, employee attrition, job roles,
departments, and employee satisfaction.

The dashboard helps HR teams identify workforce trends
and understand factors associated with employee turnover.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze total employees and employee attrition.
- Calculate the employee attrition rate.
- Analyze attrition by gender and department.
- Compare attrition across job roles and education fields.
- Understand attrition patterns across age groups.
- Analyze employee job levels and satisfaction.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel / Power BI
- Data Cleaning and Data Transformation
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts / Data Modeling and DAX
- Data Visualization

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Employees
- Employees Left
- Attrition Rate
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Age
- Average Tenure

## Key Insights

- The dashboard reports 4,410 total employees.
- A total of 711 employees have left.
- The displayed attrition rate is 16%.
- Average employee age is 37 years.
- Average employee tenure is 7 years.
- Attrition can be compared across departments,
  job roles, age groups, and education fields.

## Dashboard Features

- Interactive department and job role filters.
- Gender and business travel filters.
- Attrition analysis by employee demographics.
- Job level and satisfaction analysis.
- KPI cards for workforce monitoring.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analytics and
visualization can help HR teams monitor workforce trends,
explore employee attrition, and support data-driven decisions.
